# AIM Dissertation Research Questions and Contribution Contract

## Status of this contract

This document is the working scientific contract for the AIM dissertation. It
narrows the existing broad thesis question into four research questions and
five defensible contribution claims. The claims are candidates until the
related-work, mathematical-obligation, validation and discussion packages have
supplied their evidence.

The contract explains approved Knowledge Kernel meaning but does not establish
or modify it. Proposition identifiers refer to
`PROPOSITION_INVENTORY.md`. They identify audit obligations, not automatic
support for a claim.

## RQ1 — Identity and responsibility separation

### Question

How can a railway Alignment retain identifiable engineering continuity across
construction, realization, representation, observation, evaluation and change
without treating these responsibilities as one state?

### Falsifiable boundary

The proposed separation fails if a complete engineering operation cannot
distinguish the durable Alignment identity and revision lineage from at least
one representation, observation, realization or evaluation result; or if a
change of representation necessarily changes Alignment identity.

### Claimed novelty to be tested

AIM claims an Alignment-specific, operationally executable responsibility model
that combines durable identity with explicit relations between constructive,
realized, represented, observed, evaluated and decided states. Novelty is the
integrated Alignment-specific contract and its executable trace, not the
individual existence of identity theory, provenance, representations or
observations.

### Evaluation method

1. Compare the responsibility model with information-modelling, digital-twin,
   BIM/IFC and railway exchange approaches in DISS-03.
2. Trace representative state changes through stable identity, revision and
   provenance records.
3. Use counterexamples that deliberately substitute a file, view, measurement
   or evaluation for the Alignment and verify that admission or conformance
   checks reject the substitution.
4. Inspect the complete validation journey for identity continuity before and
   after semantic reopen.

### Proposition links

- Central scientific claims: P025, P027, P028, P050, P083, P090, P091.
- Normative obligations: P022, P023, P026, P052, P073, P080, P085, P087–P089.

### Explicit non-claims

- AIM does not introduce a general philosophical theory of identity.
- AIM does not claim that every representation or observation can be admitted
  as constructive truth.
- AIM does not make topology, CRS, measurement or evaluation part of identity
  merely because they are related to an Alignment.

## RQ2 — Executable and bounded engineering knowledge

### Question

How can applicable railway-engineering knowledge be made computationally
effective through explicit contracts while keeping calculation, applicability,
evaluation and human or governed decision authority distinct?

### Falsifiable boundary

The approach fails if a calculated consequence cannot expose its inputs,
assumptions, applicable knowledge, residuals and authority status; if an
inapplicable rule is silently applied; or if a solver result is indistinguishable
from an approved engineering decision.

### Claimed novelty to be tested

AIM claims an Alignment-specific execution-contract pattern that binds state,
context, operators and admitted knowledge while declaring both computational
effects and non-authority. Novelty is claimed for the coherent executable
boundary across the Alignment workflow, not for numerical solvers, rule engines
or constraint systems in isolation.

### Evaluation method

1. Compare execution contracts with knowledge representation, rule checking,
   constraint solving and model-based engineering in DISS-03.
2. Audit selected contracts for declared inputs, outputs, assumptions,
   applicability, residuals and what they cannot decide.
3. Run applicable, inapplicable, ambiguous and underdetermined cases and check
   for explicit terminal outcomes.
4. Demonstrate that AXTRAN candidate generation and assessment do not perform
   admission, approval or persistence by themselves.

### Proposition links

- Scientific and methodological claims: P001–P010, P020, P029, P054, P064,
  P066, P071.
- Normative/reference obligations: P026, P074–P082, P085.

### Explicit non-claims

- AIM does not prove every engineering rule universally applicable.
- AIM does not replace engineering judgment or governance.
- AIM does not claim AXTRAN2, transitionDB or any one transition family to be
  the universal source of Alignment meaning.

## RQ3 — Intrinsic constructive composition and qualified realization

### Question

Can horizontal geometry, vertical geometry, explicit left/right Rail-Pair-Cant
laws and operational chainage be constructed and evaluated on a common
intrinsic parameter while remaining distinct and being realized consistently
in planar, spatial and qualified world contexts?

### Falsifiable boundary

The model fails if two views at the same intrinsic cursor refer to different
constructive states; if midpoint, cross-level or common offset must be stored as
independent authority rather than derived from rail laws; if chainage changes
necessarily alter constructive identity; or if a qualified realization hides
CRS, metric, frame or ambiguity assumptions.

### Claimed novelty to be tested

AIM claims a unified but non-collapsing Alignment construction in which
curvature-driven horizontal state, height, Rail-Pair-Cant and chainage share an
intrinsic longitudinal reference and enter qualified realization through
explicit operators. Novelty is the controlled composition and responsibility
boundary, not the invention of curvature integration, vertical curves, cant,
stationing or geodetic projection individually.

### Evaluation method

1. Close all SCI-MATH obligations used by the composition through proof,
   bounded derivation or primary citation in DISS-04.
2. Compare transition, alignment, linear-referencing and CRS literature in
   DISS-03.
3. Evaluate synchronized plan, curvature, longitudinal, cant, chainage and
   cross-section projections at identical intrinsic positions.
4. Test degeneracies, chainage discontinuities, missing CRS evidence,
   left/right rail-law changes and semantic serialization/reopen.
5. Quantify realization differences where projected or ellipsoidal metric
   context differs from the engineering construction frame.

### Proposition links

- Mathematical obligations: P024, P051, P053, P055–P065, P067, P072, P083,
  P084.
- Scientific realization claims: P011–P019, P030–P049, P068–P071, P086.
- Boundary contracts: P027–P029, P052, P073, P085, P087–P089.

### Explicit non-claims

- AIM does not identify intrinsic distance with operational chainage or
  projected-world distance.
- AIM does not define every realization space as Riemannian or require one
  global chart.
- AIM does not promote GRA/Gleisschere evidence into profile, gradient or
  constructive cant without a separately approved domain-binding and admission
  contract.
- AIM does not claim one transition family to be universally optimal.

## RQ4 — Epistemically controlled knowledge work and preservation

### Question

Can heterogeneous railway source evidence be admitted fail-closed, used in a
consequential Alignment edit, presented through synchronized views and
persisted and reopened without silent semantic promotion or loss?

### Falsifiable boundary

The workflow fails if an unsupported source record becomes constructive truth,
if ambiguity or rejection disappears, if import or calculation lacks a terminal
state, if synchronized views cease to describe one selected revision, or if
save/close/reopen changes identity, rail laws, chainage, provenance, authority
status or calculated consequence.

### Claimed novelty to be tested

AIM claims an end-to-end Alignment knowledge-work protocol that preserves
epistemic status and engineering identity from heterogeneous evidence through
admission, calculation, review, synchronized presentation and semantic reopen.
Novelty is claimed for the closed, inspectable protocol and its meaning-
preservation evidence, not for file parsing, persistence or visualization in
isolation.

### Evaluation method

1. Execute one versioned real-data case from empty workspace through import,
   source inventory, admission, Alignment selection, radius change, AXTRAN
   consequences, synchronized projections, save, close and reopen.
2. Record accepted, rejected, unresolved, failed and cancelled outcomes.
3. Compare canonical pre-save and post-reopen semantic snapshots, including
   identity, revision, horizontal/vertical/rail-pair/chainage laws, provenance
   and authority status.
4. Inject unsupported and ambiguous evidence and verify fail-closed behavior.
5. Separate demonstrated steps from future IFC-roundtrip capability.

### Proposition links

- Scientific claims: P021, P050, P066, P090–P092.
- Normative obligations: P022, P025, P026, P028, P029, P052, P073, P080,
  P085, P087–P089.

### Explicit non-claims

- One case study does not establish universal format coverage or external
  validity for all railway administrations.
- Successful import does not prove constructive admission.
- The September journey does not demonstrate IFCalignment roundtrip; that is a
  separate Delivery-3 evaluation.
- The Reference Application is research evidence and a reference realization,
  not the definition of approved Kernel meaning.

## Contribution contract

### C1 — Alignment-specific responsibility and identity model

- **Addresses:** RQ1 and RQ4.
- **Contribution claim:** a typed relation model preserves Alignment identity
  and revision continuity while distinguishing construction, realization,
  representation, observation, evaluation and decision.
- **Falsifier:** a required workflow state cannot be assigned without category
  collapse, or semantic reopen cannot preserve the distinction.
- **Novelty boundary:** integrated Alignment-specific executable trace; not a
  new general identity or provenance theory.
- **Evidence:** related-work comparison, Kernel traceability matrix,
  counterexamples, and pre/post-reopen state comparison.
- **Propositions:** P022–P029, P050, P052, P073, P080, P083, P085, P087–P092.

### C2 — Executable knowledge-contract method

- **Addresses:** RQ2.
- **Contribution claim:** engineering transformations and evaluations become
  inspectably executable when their dependencies, applicability, residuals and
  non-authority are explicit.
- **Falsifier:** applicable and inapplicable cases cannot be distinguished or a
  result cannot be traced to its governing contract.
- **Novelty boundary:** Alignment-wide contract and responsibility pattern; not
  a novel generic solver or rule engine.
- **Evidence:** contract audit, four outcome classes, AXTRAN candidate trace and
  comparison with rule/constraint approaches.
- **Propositions:** P001–P010, P020, P026, P029, P054, P064, P066, P071,
  P074–P082.

### C3 — Intrinsic multi-band constructive model

- **Addresses:** RQ3.
- **Contribution claim (responsibility-and-preservation form, `DISS-04-DECISION-001`, Option A, approved by Uwe Falz on 2026-09-05):**
  admitted left/right rail laws retain constructive authority; midpoint,
  cross-level and common offset remain derived-only quantities; intrinsic
  position, chainage and qualified realization remain distinct; and these role
  assignments survive consequential change and semantic reopen while
  horizontal, vertical, rail-pair and chainage laws are evaluated at one
  intrinsic position.
- **Falsifier:** a derived cant quantity acquires independent authority,
  chainage or world realization collapses into intrinsic identity, synchronized
  views disagree on the selected constructive state, or a semantic reopen loses
  a role assignment.
- **Novelty boundary:** the responsibility-and-preservation claim only. No
  novelty is claimed for curvature reconstruction, a shared distance-along
  parameter, combined horizontal/vertical/cant state, explicit left/right cant
  values, named cant families or 3D pose generation; all have close prior art
  (IFC 4.3.2, Rail Topology Ontology, Seo et al. 2020).
- **Evidence:** closed mathematical obligations (DISS-04 repair package),
  synchronized cursor tests, degeneracy cases, CRS/metric qualification and
  semantic reopen. The claim is defended only if the real-data case (DISS-05)
  shows a concrete semantic failure prevented by the complete boundary and not
  already prevented by comparison systems (`DISS-04-RISK-001`).
- **Propositions:** P024, P027–P049, P051–P065, P067–P073, P083–P089.

### C4 — Fail-closed admission and epistemic-state workflow

- **Addresses:** RQ1, RQ2 and RQ4.
- **Contribution claim:** source evidence, partial knowledge, ambiguity and
  rejection can remain explicit through admission, consequential editing,
  review and persistence.
- **Falsifier:** unsupported evidence is silently promoted, non-accepted status
  is lost, or ambiguity becomes an authoritative constructive state.
- **Novelty boundary:** closed Alignment engineering workflow; not a claim that
  all source semantics are recoverable.
- **Evidence:** real dataset, negative admission cases, terminal outcome matrix,
  provenance trace and reopen comparison.
- **Propositions:** P021–P029, P050, P052, P073, P080, P085, P087–P092.

### C5 — Reproducible reference realization and validation protocol

- **Addresses:** all research questions.
- **Contribution claim:** an executable reference realization and a versioned
  end-to-end protocol make the preceding claims inspectable, falsifiable and
  repeatable at the stated scope.
- **Falsifier:** the protocol cannot be repeated from declared inputs, cannot
  expose intermediate authority states, or cannot compare semantic pre/post
  state.
- **Novelty boundary:** the artifact-supported validation of the integrated AIM
  proposal; not proof of universal implementation completeness or industrial
  generalization.
- **Evidence:** immutable code/data baseline, automated contract tests,
  recorded normal-start journey, semantic snapshot comparison and documented
  failures/limitations.
- **Propositions:** evidence obligations across P001–P092; especially P020,
  P028, P050, P066, P071, P083–P092.

## Claim-to-evidence acceptance rule

A contribution may appear as a defended dissertation result only when:

1. its research-state comparison establishes the gap and novelty boundary;
2. all linked SCI-MATH obligations are proved, derived, cited or reformulated;
3. all linked CONTRACT obligations trace to approved authority and have
   conformance evidence;
4. the stated evaluation has a reproducible artifact and observable result;
5. counterexamples and threats to validity are reported; and
6. the conclusion remains within the explicit non-claims above.

Until then, the contribution is a dissertation hypothesis or candidate, not a
demonstrated conclusion.
