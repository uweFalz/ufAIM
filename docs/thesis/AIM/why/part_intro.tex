\chapter*{Why Alignments Matter}
\phantomsection
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Introduction}

\begin{remark}
This part introduces the conceptual motivation of the AIM framework.
It explains why railway alignments cannot be treated as mere geometric
curves, but must be understood as structured engineering objects.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}
The central claim is that alignment modelling must integrate geometry,
dynamics, realization, and data interpretation within a single coherent
framework.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}
The following chapters therefore develop the problem setting, the need
for a new perspective, and the guiding vision behind
alignment-based information modelling.
\end{remark}
