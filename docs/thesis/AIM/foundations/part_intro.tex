% \chapter{Mathematical Foundations}

\begin{remark}
This part establishes the mathematical foundation of the AIM framework.
Its purpose is not to develop abstract mathematics for its own sake,
but to identify the intrinsic structures underlying railway alignment
modelling.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}
In particular, the focus lies on arc-length based descriptions,
curvature-driven geometry, and the distinction between primary and
derived quantities.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}
These foundations provide the conceptual and formal basis for all later
developments in modelling, realization, and optimization.
\end{remark}
