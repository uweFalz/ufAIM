\chapter{Axiomatic Foundation}
\label{chap:axiomatic-foundation}

\index{Axiom}
\index{Geometry!intrinsic planar}

The mathematical development of this thesis begins with the intrinsic
planar geometry of an alignment. Let \(I\subseteq\mathbb{R}\) be an
interval parameterized by arc-length \(s\). The alignment curve,
heading, and curvature are denoted by
\[
\gamma:I\rightarrow\mathbb{R}^{2},
\qquad
\theta:I\rightarrow\mathbb{R},
\qquad
\kappa:I\rightarrow\mathbb{R}.
\]

% ============================================================
\section{Intrinsic Planar Geometry}
% ============================================================

\begin{axiom}[Arc-length Parameterization]
\label{ax:arc-length-parameterization}
\index{Arc-length parameterization}
The alignment curve is parameterized by arc-length:
\[
\lVert\gamma'(s)\rVert=1
\qquad\text{for all }s\in I.
\]
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Heading Evolution]
\label{ax:heading-evolution}
\index{Heading evolution}
\index{Curvature!as derivative of heading}
Curvature is the derivative of heading with respect to arc-length:
\[
\theta'(s)=\kappa(s).
\]
\end{axiom}

\begin{axiom}[Tangent Reconstruction]
\label{ax:tangent-reconstruction}
\index{Tangent reconstruction}
\index{Reconstruction!tangent}
The unit tangent of the planar alignment is determined by the heading:
\[
\gamma'(s)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\theta(s)\\
\sin\theta(s)
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
\end{axiom}

% ============================================================
\section{Reconstruction Consequence}
% ============================================================

\begin{proposition}[Curvature-driven reconstruction]
\label{prop:curvature-driven-reconstruction}
\index{Reconstruction!curvature-driven}
Given an initial position \(\gamma(s_0)\), an initial heading
\(\theta(s_0)\), and a sufficiently regular curvature function
\(\kappa\), the planar alignment is uniquely determined on \(I\).
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Integration of \cref{ax:heading-evolution} determines
\[
\theta(s)=\theta(s_0)+\int_{s_0}^{s}\kappa(\sigma)\,\dd\sigma.
\]
Substitution into \cref{ax:tangent-reconstruction} and subsequent
integration determine
\[
\gamma(s)=\gamma(s_0)
+
\int_{s_0}^{s}
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos\theta(\sigma)\\
\sin\theta(\sigma)
\end{pmatrix}
\dd\sigma.
\]
The prescribed initial conditions therefore fix the remaining
integration constants.
\end{proof}

These axioms establish the intrinsic planar reconstruction kernel used
throughout the subsequent development. Architectural separation,
realization, and representation are governed by the principles of the
following chapter rather than by additional mathematical axioms.
\index{Kernel!intrinsic planar reconstruction}
