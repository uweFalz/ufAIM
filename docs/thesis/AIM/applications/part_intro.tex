\chapter*{Applications}
\phantomsection
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Applications}

The preceding parts established approved engineering concepts for
alignment semantics, state, runtime evaluation, and reality coupling.
The Applications part demonstrates what this architecture enables in
concrete engineering contexts.

Applications therefore follow the progression
\[
\text{approved engineering concepts}
\rightarrow
\text{application context}
\rightarrow
\text{engineering use case}
\rightarrow
\text{required runtime/realization services}
\rightarrow
\text{derived representation or decision support}
\rightarrow
\text{engineering consequence}.
\]

Application chapters consume canonical concepts and do not become new
conceptual homes for alignment identity, pose2/pose3, realization, or
representation layers.

engineering consequence while preserving conceptual ownership in
