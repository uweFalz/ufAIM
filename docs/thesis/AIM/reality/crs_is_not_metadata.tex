\section*{CRS Is Not Metadata}

In railway alignment modelling, CRS cannot be treated as auxiliary
metadata. CRS assumptions affect metric interpretation, coordinate
transformations, projection behaviour, and comparison of target and
observed states.

\begin{principle}[CRS structural principle]
CRS is a structural component of realization and embedding context.
Ignoring it breaks consistency of engineering interpretation.
\end{principle}

Consequently, CRS handling must be explicit in import, runtime
projection, reconstruction, and realization-aware comparison workflows.
