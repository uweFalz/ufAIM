\chapter{Optimization of Alignments}

This chapter bundle answers how alignment design and reconstruction are
formulated as optimization problems within AIM. It establishes a
continuous-to-discrete progression from problem context, to curvature-
space functionals, to discrete formulations, to SQP-oriented solution
architecture.

The sequence is intentionally cumulative: each included section reuses
the previous result and adds one layer of formal and engineering
specificity.
