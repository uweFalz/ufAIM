## Main correction to the baseline

The German development is not a single succession from textbook to regulation
to CAD. At least four partially independent lines must be tracked:

1. legal minimum rules for construction and operation;
2. railway-administration design and maintenance rules;
3. textbooks and specialist geometry;
4. surveying, kilometre referencing and computational production systems.

Their vocabularies overlap, but their authority, purpose and historical cadence
do not.
