## Highest-value archive requests

1. **Landesarchiv Berlin:** `A Rep. 080-04 Nr. 929`, DV 820 (1928), including
   supplements, with permission for scholarly page notes.
2. **Bundesarchiv / DB Museum:** edition registers and change notices for
   DS 877, DS 800, Ril 800 and Ril 883.
3. **DB/DR technical libraries:** DS/DV 820 editions valid 1969 and DR 1977,
   compared by paragraph and formula.
4. **DNB/Transpress copy:** *Linienführung der Eisenbahn* (1966), title page,
   introduction, transition, cant, vertical-alignment and computation chapters.
5. **Journal archive:** Krause 1990, *Vermessungstechnik* 38(8), pp. 260–261.
6. **DB computing archives/oral history:** mainframe alignment programs,
   punched-card inputs, plotters, file formats, organizational ownership and
   migration into CAD.

## Done criterion for a future archive mission

For one selected rule family, produce an edition table containing validity
date, publisher/administration, paragraph structure, changed formulas,
supersession evidence, page images or archive notes where lawful, and explicit
negative results. Do not infer semantic continuity from an unchanged document
number.
