## 4. Work Performed

Research question:

What cannot be removed from a present Railway Alignment without losing a
meaningful engineering action?

Tests:

- removal test for each of eight requested dimensions;
- counterexamples using polyline, H/V/C exchange, kilometre table, topological
  edge, georeferenced axis, compliance flag, and serialized runtime graph;
- historical confrontation against surveying, transition, cant/speed,
  vertical, kilometre, computation, topology and BIM developments;
- standards confrontation against IFC 4.3.2.0, LandInfra/InfraGML, ISO 19148,
  EN 13803 metadata, UIC RTM/RSM and ERA RINF;
- ufAIM comparison against active Kernel boundaries and current RefImpl slices.

Result:

- universal/domain-established responsibilities are separated from ufAIM
  synthesis candidates and open gaps;
- explicit zero, unknown, local-only and disconnected states are part of
  minimal completeness;
- speed, CRS, kilometre, topology, evaluation and authority do not become
  intrinsic identity merely because the working aggregate coordinates them;
- concrete successor packages are defined for App-Core, Knowledge Kernel and
  Thesis.

Confidence:

- high for necessity and role separation of the major domain dimensions;
- medium-high for the four-part nucleus;
- medium to medium-low for exact persistence and identity-survival rules.
