## Transfer rule

A source can enter the later Thesis only when:

- its bibliographic identity is complete enough to resolve the item;
- the cited page or section was actually inspected;
- the claim does not exceed what that page supports;
- catalogue metadata is not presented as content evidence;
- novelty language remains hypothetical unless a separate prior-art study
  supports it.
