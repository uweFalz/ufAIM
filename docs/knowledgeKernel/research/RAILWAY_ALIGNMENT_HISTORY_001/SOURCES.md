## Source-use rules for follow-up work

1. Cite scan title pages, not search snippets, for author/edition/publisher.
2. Cite exact pages for formulas or priority claims.
3. Separate first proposal, first publication, first construction, first
   institutional adoption and first mandatory standard.
4. Do not infer railway adoption from a road-design source, or vice versa.
5. Treat catalogues as discovery/provenance evidence, not substitutes for
   reading.
6. Record access date and standard status because current/withdrawn metadata can
   change.
