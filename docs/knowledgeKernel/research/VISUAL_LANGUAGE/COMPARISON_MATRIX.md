## Decision

- **Primary: Intrinsic Spine.** Highest combined cover impact, domain specificity, and semantic economy.
- **Reserve: Kernel Loom.** Most accurate compact architecture when the Kernel’s institutional position is the subject.
- **Rejected for cover but retained: Evidence Aperture.** Excellent explanatory system, but the directional flow can be mistaken for a mandatory universal workflow and authority is still only partly canonicalized.
