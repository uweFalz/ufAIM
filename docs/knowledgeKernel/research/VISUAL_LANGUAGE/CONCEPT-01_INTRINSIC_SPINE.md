# Direction 1 — Intrinsic Spine

**Thesis:** The Kernel preserves the intrinsic constructive line—ordered by `s` and shaped by curvature—while every visible geometry remains a contextual realization.

## Metaphor and truthfulness

A single forceful railway-derived spine crosses the page. Small transverse marks establish intrinsic order; changing curvature makes the line unmistakably an Alignment rather than an abstract divider. Two faint offset traces are metric realizations, not alternative identities. A compact square “datum” at the center marks the Kernel’s preserving responsibility without turning it into a database.

This maps directly to `KC-ID-004` and `KC-REALIZATION-001`: intrinsic parameterization and curvature belong to Alignment Identity; coordinates and measurable geometry arise under explicit realization context.

## Composition

- **Foreground:** black intrinsic spine, ordered station ticks, central Kernel datum, one topology junction.
- **Background:** two thin realization traces and a sparse coordinate field.
- **Absent deliberately:** train silhouette, rails/sleepers, map tiles, software layers, governance flow, literal equations.
- **A4 portrait:** title occupies the quiet upper quarter; spine enters low left, crosses the datum near optical center, and exits upper right. The subtitle sits below the datum without touching the line.

## Color and monochrome

- Base: warm paper `#F4F0E8`, graphite `#15181B`.
- Identity/accent: signal vermilion `#D64A32`.
- Realization/context: deep cyan `#167C80` at reduced opacity.
- Monochrome: intrinsic line 100% K, datum 75% K, realization traces 30% and 15% K; dotted/solid distinctions retain meaning.

## Typography

Use a non-proprietary humanist sans or the Thesis’s Latin Modern Sans for the compact title; use Latin Modern Roman/system serif for subtitle and author. Type remains subordinate to the spine. English and German titles occupy the same two-line block.

## Strengths, risks, and likely misreadings

- **Strengths:** strongest thumbnail, railway-specific without illustration, memorable single gesture, excellent icon crop.
- **Risk:** may be read as “the Kernel is one alignment.”
- **Mitigation:** the central datum and multiple faint realizations signal that Alignment is the exemplar, not the complete Kernel inventory.
- **Likely misreading:** faint offsets as design alternatives; chapter captions must call them realizations under different contexts.

## System extension

- Chapter openers select one tick and add one short domain label.
- Diagrams reuse the line hierarchy: heavy intrinsic, medium operator, light realization/representation.
- App identity uses the datum plus a cropped curvature inflection.
- Icons use station, context, observation, or authority marks attached to the same spine; no decorative line proliferation.

## Explicit semantic mapping

| Visible element | Kernel concept |

| Heavy curved spine | Alignment Identity: intrinsic ordered construction and curvature evolution (`KC-ID-004`) |
