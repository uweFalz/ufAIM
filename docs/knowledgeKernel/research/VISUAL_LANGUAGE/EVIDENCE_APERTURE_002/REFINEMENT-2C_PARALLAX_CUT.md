# 2C — Parallax Cut

**One-sentence reading order:** Read the heavy alignment on the lower-left plane, cross the narrow aperture cut with the observation, then encounter a displaced representation on the upper-right plane and a detached human authority above both.

## Explicit Kernel mapping

| Visible element | Kernel meaning |
|---|---|
| Lower-left teal alignment | intended/physical subject |
| Narrow white diagonal cut | explicit semantic/domain boundary |
| Ochre witness and short crossing ray | observation with provenance crossing the boundary |
| Upper-right graphite echo | representation/evaluable state, displaced from rather than substituted for the subject |
| Mismatched endpoints across the cut | no assertion of identity between physical and represented geometry |
| Detached human seal in negative space | accountable authority outside both reality and representation planes |
| Red stop bar beneath seal | evaluation cannot pass automatically into authority |

## What became more self-explanatory

Reality and representation no longer share one ray or plane. Their parallax displacement makes category separation immediate, while the observation is the only mark allowed to cross the aperture. The large diagonal cut gives the page greater dissertation-cover tension.

## Remaining likely misreading

The design can look like a prism, portal, or before/after transformation in which the alignment itself changes. The short crossing ray mitigates this, but the concept remains less self-explanatory than 2A and would need a concise subtitle in final use.

## Assessment

- **A4:** highest formal tension and most editorial negative space.
- **Thumbnail:** strong silhouette; semantic detail is less obvious than 2A.
- **Color:** very clear plane separation.
- **Grayscale:** passes through opposing line weights, the white cut, and the detached double-ring seal.
