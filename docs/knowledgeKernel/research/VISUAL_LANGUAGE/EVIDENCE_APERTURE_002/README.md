# Evidence Aperture — Iteration 002

Status: Research; non-canonical; preserves all original Visual Language concepts and prototypes.

## Recommendation

**Select 2A — Unbroken Subject for final Thesis-artwork development.**

It resolves the first prototype’s principal ambiguity with one visual fact: the heavy alignment subject continues visibly and unchanged behind the aperture, while only a witnessed observation branches upward, is framed, and becomes a bounded representation. The detached human seal remains beyond a deliberate gap. At thumbnail size the reading is still: **subject continues → evidence branches → framed record emerges → responsible authority remains separate**.

## Exactly three refinements

1. [2A — Unbroken Subject](REFINEMENT-2A_UNBROKEN_SUBJECT.md): the subject is continuous; evidence branches from it.
2. [2B — Witness Frame](REFINEMENT-2B_WITNESS_FRAME.md): plural observations converge on a frame that contains a representation, not the subject.
3. [2C — Parallax Cut](REFINEMENT-2C_PARALLAX_CUT.md): physical and represented traces occupy visibly different planes separated by a narrow aperture.

These are compositional alternatives, not color variants. All use the same semantic color roles solely to make structural comparison fair:

- teal: intended/physical alignment subject;
- ochre: observation and provenance;
- graphite: representation/evaluation;
- vermilion: separately responsible human authority.

Shape and separation carry the same distinctions in grayscale.

## Evidence guardrails

- Alignment carries intrinsic longitudinal order and curvature evolution: `IDENTITY/KC-ID-004_Alignment_Identity.md`.
- Metric Realization supplies measurable interpretation without replacing identity: `REALIZATION/KC-REALIZATION-001_Metric_Realization.md`.
- Physical realization, physical state, observation, and representation remain distinct: `REALIZATION/KC-REALIZATION-005_Physical_Realization.md`.
- Observation includes provenance and is evidence, not reality or accepted knowledge: `EVALUATION/KC-EVAL-002_Engineering_Observation.md`.
- Representation does not become Identity: approved `FREEZES/FC-001.md`.
- Evaluation does not exercise decision authority: established boundary in `EVALUATION/KC-EVAL-011_Engineering_Decision.md`.

The prototypes therefore show no arrow from evaluation into authority, no “truth” badge, no automatic acceptance, and no transformation of the heavy subject line itself.
