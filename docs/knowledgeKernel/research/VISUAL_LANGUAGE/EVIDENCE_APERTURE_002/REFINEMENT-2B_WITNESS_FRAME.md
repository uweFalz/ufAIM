# 2B — Witness Frame

**One-sentence reading order:** Multiple provenance-bearing observations rise from the alignment into a large empty frame, where only their reconciled representation appears, while authority watches from outside the frame.

## Explicit Kernel mapping

| Visible element | Kernel meaning |
|---|---|
| Low heavy teal alignment | intended/physical subject and railway identity cue |
| Three witness stems with unequal origins | plural, potentially differing observations |
| Bead patterns on stems | retained provenance and acquisition context |
| Large open graphite frame | context-qualified representation boundary; not a container for reality |
| Thin curve inside the frame | represented/evaluable information |
| Paired short bars beside the represented curve | comparison/residual without claiming a decision |
| Human seal outside the frame | authority is not produced by representation or evaluation |
| Empty space between frame and seal | explicit non-automatic passage |

## What became more self-explanatory

Plural observations now visibly originate at distinct points on one alignment and converge toward a frame. Because the heavy alignment never enters the frame, the frame cannot easily be mistaken for changing or containing reality. The thin curve inside is visibly a representation of a different graphical order.

## Remaining likely misreading

Converging observation stems may imply that multiple sources are always reconciled into one representation. The final system should allow conflicting traces or an empty frame when evidence is insufficient.

## Assessment

- **A4:** calm and intellectually serious, but less dramatic than 2A.
- **Thumbnail:** passes, though the provenance beads reduce to texture rather than individually readable marks.
- **Color:** strongest observation/provenance emphasis.
- **Grayscale:** passes because stems differ in pattern and the frame/subject use sharply different weights.
