# 2A — Unbroken Subject

**One-sentence reading order:** Follow the uninterrupted alignment, see a witnessed observation branch through the aperture into a bounded comparison sheet, then notice that human authority remains across an unbridged gap.

## Explicit Kernel mapping

| Visible element | Kernel meaning |
|---|---|
| Heavy teal S-curve crossing the entire page | intended/physical alignment subject; its continuity denies that observation changes reality |
| White transverse marks | intrinsic longitudinal order, not operational station labels |
| Ochre witness ring touching the subject | an observation encounter with a subject |
| Three small ochre provenance beads | source, method/time/conditions carried with the observation |
| Branching ochre ray | evidence derived from the encounter; not the subject itself |
| Open square aperture | explicit context and applicability boundary |
| Graphite sheet with two traces | bounded representation and evaluation/comparison |
| Sheet stopping below the seal | evaluation does not exercise authority |
| Detached vermilion human seal | separately responsible authority; no automatic decision |

## What became more self-explanatory

The first prototype’s lower subject and diagonal evidence ray could look unrelated. Here the observation visibly begins at a witness point on the subject, while the subject itself continues underneath the entire composition. The framed output is unmistakably sheet-like and contains a comparison, so the aperture reads as framing evidence rather than altering reality.

## Remaining likely misreading

The aperture may still be read as a camera or scanner. This is acceptable if the final artwork keeps the explicit gap to authority and avoids lens highlights, photographic framing, or “capture” language.

## Assessment

- **A4:** strongest hierarchy of the three; one dominant curve plus one sharp rising branch creates cover tension without a process-chart grid.
- **Thumbnail:** passes; subject, aperture, sheet, and detached seal remain four distinct masses.
- **Color:** category roles are immediate and limited to four stable hues.
- **Grayscale:** passes through line weight, bead sequence, open frame, bounded sheet, and double-ring seal.
