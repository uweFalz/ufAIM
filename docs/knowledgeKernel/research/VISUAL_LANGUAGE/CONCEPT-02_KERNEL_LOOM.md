# Direction 2 — Kernel Loom

**Thesis:** The Kernel is the stable semantic warp through which Research is admitted and from which Thesis explanation and Reference Implementation correspondence are woven without becoming authority.

## Metaphor and truthfulness

Three horizontal channels enter a tall central datum. Research arrives from the left as heterogeneous strands. Inside the datum, strands are reduced to four stable responsibilities—Identity, Realization, Observation/Evaluation, and Authority. Two disciplined outputs leave to the right: Thesis and RefImpl. The image is a loom, not a pipeline: the central object preserves relationships; it does not manufacture truth automatically.

The model follows `docs/knowledgeKernel/README.md` and the Constitution’s responsibility sentence. It avoids making Research, Thesis, or RefImpl part of the active Kernel.

## Composition

- **Foreground:** one tall vertical Kernel bar and four crossing semantic channels.
- **Background:** minimal registration grid and one alignment-curvature motif embedded in the Identity channel.
- **Absent deliberately:** boxes for every concept, arrows between concept files, technical stack layers, approval status counts.
- **A4 portrait:** the Kernel bar is slightly right of center; Research strands approach from the lower left; Thesis and RefImpl leave toward the upper and lower right. Title sits top left.

## Color and monochrome

- Base: near-white `#F7F6F2`, carbon `#202327`.
- Identity: vermilion `#D64A32`.
- Realization: teal `#247B7B`.
- Observation/evaluation: ochre `#C58A22`.
- Authority: near-black.
- Monochrome: channels use distinct line patterns and widths; labels remain on-channel, making color optional.

## Typography

Neutral non-proprietary grotesk/system sans for labels; Latin Modern Roman or system serif for the dissertation title. Labels are nouns, never status slogans. German compounds fit by allowing labels to rotate 90° within the datum only in the final typeset version.

## Strengths, risks, and likely misreadings

- **Strengths:** clearest Kernel/Research/Thesis/RefImpl relationship; modular diagram language; excellent grayscale.
- **Risk:** architectural bars may feel institutional or bureaucratic.
- **Likely misreading:** a one-way ETL pipeline or four approved Kernel layers.
- **Mitigation:** channels cross rather than terminate, and the documentation states these are visual responsibilities, not an invented hierarchy.

## System extension

- Chapter openers isolate one channel while retaining the central datum.
- Architecture diagrams reuse channel patterns and the “outside/inside/outside” boundary.
- App identity can use the central datum with one curvature channel, but the mark is less railway-specific than Direction 1.
- Small icons derive from channel/datum intersections, not miniature flowcharts.

## Explicit semantic mapping

| Visible element | Kernel concept |
|---|---|
| Incoming irregular strands | Research evidence and unresolved hypotheses |
| Central vertical datum | Knowledge Kernel’s preserving, bounded authority |
| Identity channel with curve | Engineering Object and Alignment Identity (`KC-ID-*`) |
| Realization channel | explicit Metric/Physical Realization distinctions (`KC-REALIZATION-*`) |
| Observation/evaluation channel | evidence, residual, constraint, and evaluation responsibilities (`KC-EVAL-*`) |
| Authority channel | admitted knowledge and Governance boundary; not solver authority |
| Upper outgoing line | Thesis explains Kernel meaning |
| Lower outgoing line | Reference Implementation demonstrates/corresponds to Kernel meaning |
| Open ends | neither Thesis nor RefImpl closes or defines the Kernel |
