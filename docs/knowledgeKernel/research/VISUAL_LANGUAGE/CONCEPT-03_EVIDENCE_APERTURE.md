# Direction 3 — Evidence Aperture

**Thesis:** The Kernel keeps reality, representation, evidence, evaluation, and authority apart by making every transformation cross an explicit semantic aperture.

## Metaphor and truthfulness

A physical alignment-like trace occupies the lower plane. An observation ray rises through a diamond aperture, becoming a bounded representation and then an evaluable statement. A separate authority mark sits beyond evaluation; the ray stops before it. The composition makes the critical Kernel boundaries visible: observation is not reality, representation is not identity, and evaluation is not decision.

This is grounded in `FC-001/002`, `KC-REALIZATION-005`, `KC-EVAL-002`, and the established boundary in `KC-EVAL-011`.

## Composition

- **Foreground:** diagonal evidence ray, central aperture, separated authority seal.
- **Background:** lower physical trace and upper representation plane.
- **Absent deliberately:** automatic arrow into “decision,” generic database cylinders, truth gauges, photographic reality, invented scoring.
- **A4 portrait:** the diagonal rises from lower left to upper right; aperture occupies the visual center; the title sits in the upper-left negative space and the authority seal remains visibly separate.

## Color and monochrome

- Base: cool paper `#F3F4F2`, charcoal `#1B1E22`.
- Physical/realization: teal `#167C80`.
- Observation/evidence: ochre `#C58A22`.
- Authority boundary: vermilion `#D64A32`.
- Monochrome: physical plane 30% K, evidence ray 80% K, aperture white with black outline, authority seal double-outline; separation survives photocopying.

## Typography

Use Latin Modern Roman/system serif for the title to counterbalance the geometric aperture; use a plain non-proprietary sans for the four semantic labels. Labels must remain replaceable for German without changing the composition.

## Strengths, risks, and likely misreadings

- **Strengths:** strongest conceptual tension; excellent for explaining category boundaries; naturally extends into animated or staged diagrams.
- **Risks:** can imply a universal linear workflow and can overstate the still evidence-missing Engineering Decision concept.
- **Likely misreading:** the aperture “certifies truth.”
- **Mitigation:** the prototype labels it “explicit context,” leaves competing rays visible, and stops evaluation before the authority seal.
- **Cover disposition:** rejected for the title page because process dominates identity.

## System extension

- Chapter openers place the aperture at the relevant boundary: world/track, intent/physical, observation/knowledge, candidate/authority.
- Diagrams use ray breaks to show non-applicability, ambiguity, or multiple results.
- App identity is possible as a diamond aperture, but it would need accompanying alignment cues.
- Icons can encode observation, representation, evaluation, and authority with a common aperture grammar.

## Explicit semantic mapping

| Visible element | Kernel concept |
|---|---|
| Lower curved trace | intended/physical alignment subject, deliberately not equated with its observation |
| Multiple faint rays | plural and potentially conflicting observations |
| Gold evidence ray | source-grounded Engineering Observation with provenance (`KC-EVAL-002`) |
| Diamond aperture | explicit Realization Context/applicability and semantic boundary |
| Upper bounded plane | representation/evaluable information, not identity or reality (`FC-001`) |
| Residual gap | evaluation compares without becoming observation uncertainty or physical variation |
| Detached double-circle seal | accountable authority remains separate; evaluation does not decide (`KC-EVAL-011`) |
| Ray stopping before seal | no automatic promotion from evidence or solver result to decision |
