# GAP2-D003 Exception Search

## Question

Do publicly accessible rule or practice sources establish further engineering
exceptions beyond explicitly justified undertiefung at curved switches?

Search date: 2026-07-26

## Result

`No further exception established.`

The search supports the widespread source/rule convention that the outer rail
is raised relative to the inner rail. This is not evidence for using the lower
rail as the ufAIM/AIM-Core working trajectory. The search also supports treating
cant in curved switches as a special, constrained engineering situation. It did
not establish another engineering exception.

Consequently:

- undertiefung at a Bogenweiche remains the sole currently recognized
  professional exception under Uwe Falz’s decision;
- negative cant values, changing source references, or unusual rail
  arrangements do not become additional engineering exceptions by observation
  alone;
- they remain `Unknown`, `Ambiguous`, or
  `requiresProfessionalReview` until a reliable rule/practice source and exact
  applicability are verified.

## Evidence

### EX-01 — German legal ordinary rule

[ESBO § 6(3)](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/esbo/__6.html) states for
curves in continuous main tracks that the outer rail must generally be higher
than the inner rail.

Use:

- supports the widespread outer-higher/inner-lower source convention;
- does not define the ufAIM/AIM-Core working trajectory;
- the phrase “in der Regel” is not sufficient to invent unspecified
  exceptions.

### EX-02 — Swiss official curved-switch rules

[Swiss BAV, AB-EBV, Art. 17, section 6.3](https://www.bav.admin.ch/dam/bav/de/dokumente/ab-ebv/eisenbahn/a2016/ab-ebv-a2016.pdf.download.pdf/AB-EBV.pdf)
identifies Bogenweichen as unfavorable in dynamic and maintenance terms and
imposes special cant restrictions on main and diverging branches.

Use:

- confirms that curved switches require explicit switch/branch context;
- supports exception-specific modeling rather than sign inference;
- the inspected search excerpt does not establish a general alternative height
  datum and is not used to create another exception.

### EX-03 — Public secondary indication of undertiefung

Public secondary descriptions report under-deepened diverging tracks of
outside-curved switches. This is consistent with Uwe Falz’s professional
decision but is not treated as sufficient rule-level evidence for formula,
limit, or universal applicability.

Use:

- orientation only;
- no technical claim is promoted from the secondary source.

## Negative searches

Queries included:

- `Bogenweiche Untertiefung Überhöhung Schiene Regelwerk`
- `Bogenweichen Untertiefung bogeninnere Schiene Eisenbahn`
- official DB-domain searches;
- university-domain searches.

Not established:

- another named datum exception;
- a general permission to anchor the outer rail;
- permission to infer undertiefung from cant sign;
- universal applicability across all switch types;
- a public DB primary rule specifying the complete undertiefung contract.

## Research rule

Future Research may add an exception only when it records:

1. authoritative or professionally reliable source;
2. exact railway and asset context;
3. referenced rail/side and distance definition;
4. amount or construction law;
5. applicability and validity;
6. provenance;
7. why the case is not merely incomplete or contradictory source data.

Until then, no additional exception enters the contract.
