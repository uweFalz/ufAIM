## 4. Work Performed

Research question: what must mainView and Cockpit become to make alignmentOS
operable like a domain-specific Finder while preserving engineering identity,
realization, evidence, applicability, provenance, and authority distinctions?

Tests performed:

- Tested one global selection against Alignment editing, transition catalogue
  browsing, import evidence, calculation candidates, and derived objects.
- Tested local-only work versus later geographic context.
- Tested Finder principles against engineering counterexamples: multiple true
  representations, calculated-but-unapplied output, unresolved evidence,
  dependent objects, and explicit authority.
- Walked complete New Alignment, Imported Alignment, Transition Calculation,
  and Use Without Editing scenarios through surfaces, mutations, undo, evidence,
  and authority boundaries.
- Tested the derived-object model with an exterior platform, category IV,
  usable length 200 m, without inventing platform rules.
- Compared primary documentation for Finder, ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Civil 3D, Revit,
  Blender, ParaView, and Google Earth interaction patterns. Primary sources:
  [Apple Finder](https://support.apple.com/en-ca/guide/mac-mini/apddf030866a/mac),
  [ArcGIS Pro linked views](https://doc.esri.com/en/arcgis-pro/latest/help/mapping/navigation/link-multiple-views.html),
  [QGIS general tools](https://docs.qgis.org/4.2/en/docs/user_manual/introduction/general_tools.html),
  [Civil 3D profile views](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2024/ENG/Civil3D-UserGuide/files/GUID-C2C782D2-899C-4B0B-9E61-9D72A80AFC80.htm),
  [Revit Properties palette](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/2026/ENU/Revit-GetStarted/files/GUID-A764EA7A-FE26-469B-857C-F3A70812FC34.htm),
  [Blender modifiers](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/grease_pencil/modifiers/introduction.html),
  [ParaView User's Guide](https://docs.paraview.org/_/downloads/en/latest/pdf/),
  and [Google Earth navigation](https://support.google.com/earth/answer/148186?hl=en-en).

Counterexamples:

- A file/folder hierarchy cannot express intrinsic/spatial structure or
  evidence/candidate/admission distinctions.
- A property palette becomes a permanent dump if Cockpit owns exhaustive data.
- View-local selections conflict when plan, curvature, evidence, and catalogue
  panels all claim primacy.
- A generic Apply metaphor is unsafe if it implies engineering acceptance or an
  accountable decision.
- A geographic map cannot be the universal home because valid local work may
  precede CRS/geographic context.

Result:

- One typed workspace selection plus context, focus, ephemeral hover/preview,
  and labelled tool-local selection covers all required subjects.
- Representation primacy follows the active engineering question; views
  synchronize by semantic identity and intrinsic focus, not screen coordinates.
- Home is the recoverable overview of the loaded object universe in the best
  available plane.
- mainView owns framing, view mode, spatial/intrinsic selection feedback, and
  preview; Cockpit owns concise contextual orientation and routing only.
- Viewing, editing, calculation, comparison, derivation, transfer, and apply are
  distinct verbs with explicit mutation and undo boundaries.
- Truth uses five disclosure levels from immediate state to raw source.
- Alignment-based applications create dependent objects with explicit governing
  relations and change-impact state.

Confidence: high for the interaction architecture and scenario coherence;
medium for candidate/decision wording and derived-object lifecycle because the
active Kernel explicitly records unresolved evidence in those areas.

## 8. Conflicts, Risks, and Open Decisions

Contradictions and stop conditions: None. mainView and Cockpit do not require
mutually exclusive models; the selection model covers both Alignment editing
and evidence inspection; no required engineering distinction must be hidden.

Open decisions:

- `UI-OD-001`: Candidate Solution identity/state/authority remains
  evidence-missing in active `KC-EVAL-008`. Options: retain cautious UI-only
  state labels; commission focused Evaluation Research; or defer candidate
  persistence semantics. Recommended: cautious labels plus focused Research.
- `UI-OD-002`: accountable Engineering Decision semantics remain
  evidence-missing in active `KC-EVAL-011`. Options: keep Apply explicitly
  non-decisional; add a separately governed decision workflow later; or prohibit
  decision recording in the first App packages. Recommended: first and third
  until Governance resolves the concept.
- `UI-OD-003`: no promoted active Representation concept exists. Options:
  implement view synchronization strictly as App behavior; commission
  Representation Research; or seek promotion now. Recommended: App behavior
  plus separate Research, with no canonical claim.
- `UI-OD-004`: derived-platform rules and invalidation policy are outside this
  mission. Options: create a generic dependency framework first; build a
  platform-specific domain package first; or postpone derived applications.
  Recommended: generic framework with a rule-free platform demonstrator.

Risks:

- Copying panel layouts without implementing semantic selection would recreate
  competing selections.
- Colour-only state or a single validity badge would collapse uncertainty,
  applicability, acceptance, and authority.
- Map-first implementation could make valid local work feel degraded.
- “Apply” terminology may be overread as accountable acceptance unless target,
  scope, and authority text remain explicit.
- External evidence is documentation-based and needs later workflow testing
  with representative users.
