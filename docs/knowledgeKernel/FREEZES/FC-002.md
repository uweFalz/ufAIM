# FC-002 Metric Realization Is Not Identity

## Status

`approved`

## Canonical Freeze

Metric Realization is not Identity.

## Decision

Approved by Uwe Falz on 2026-07-19 through [`KD-2026-004`](../GOVERNANCE/DECISION_LOG.md#kd-2026-004).

## Meaning

A metric realization may embed, evaluate, or derive geometric or numerical form from an engineering object, but the realization does not define or become that object's intrinsic identity.
