# FC-001 Representation Is Never Identity

## Status

`approved`

## Canonical Freeze

Representation is never Identity.

## Decision

Approved by Uwe Falz on 2026-07-19 through [`KD-2026-003`](../GOVERNANCE/DECISION_LOG.md#kd-2026-003).

## Meaning

A representation may describe, encode, serialize, display, or exchange an engineering object, but it does not define or become that object's intrinsic identity.
