# KC-EDITOR-001 Canonical Narrative

## Status
candidate

## Canonical Question
How should the Thesis explain Kernel knowledge without collapsing provenance, authority, or unresolved research?

## Candidate Canonical Answer
The Thesis explains approved and candidate Kernel knowledge as one coherent canonical narrative organized by conceptual dependency, not by discovery chronology. This editorial narrative preserves provenance links to Research and does not absorb unresolved Research claims into canonical meaning.

## Normative Meaning
- Canonical explanation in the Thesis is an editorial organization of Kernel meaning.
- The explanatory order follows conceptual dependency so prerequisites are established before dependent concepts.
- A coherent narrative does not erase Research history; provenance remains explicit and separately traceable.
- Narrative authority is subordinate to Kernel authority; only Governance decisions can approve or revoke Kernel meaning.
- Unresolved or conflicting Research remains distinguishable from canonical explanation and is not silently promoted.

## Boundaries
- This concept defines editorial exposition behavior only; it does not define engineering identity, realization, evaluation, or software architecture.
- This concept does not replace Governance authority, Decision Records, or Kernel status transitions.
- This concept does not require one historical narrative; it requires one coherent canonical explanatory narrative.
- Research remains the source of alternatives, hypotheses, and unresolved findings.
