# Knowledge Kernel Constitution (Draft)

## Purpose

The alignmentOS Knowledge Kernel is the canonical representation of stable engineering knowledge.

Research is intentionally free to evolve.
The Knowledge Kernel records only concepts that have passed governance.

Knowledge evolves through the following lifecycle:

Research
↓
Summary
↓
Governance Review
↓
Approved Kernel Concept
↓
Knowledge Kernel
↓
Services
↓
Reference Implementations
↓
Thesis

The Knowledge Kernel is therefore neither an implementation nor a thesis.

It is the canonical knowledge foundation shared by both.

## Principles

- Research discovers.
- Governance canonizes.
- The Knowledge Kernel preserves.
- Services operationalize.
- Reference implementations demonstrate.
- The Thesis explains.
